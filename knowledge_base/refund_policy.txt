DemoKart Refund Policy
Customer refund terms and procedures

1. Refund Eligibility
A refund may be provided when a customer is eligible under the applicable product and order policy. Eligibility may depend on the reason for the request, product category, return eligibility, and item condition.

For an eligible return, the refund is generally processed after the returned product has been received and the required return or verification process has been completed. In situations where a return is not required, the applicable refund may be processed without receiving the product back.

2. Full Refund
An eligible customer may receive a full refund when the return satisfies the applicable policy requirements. The final refund amount and eligible components are determined according to the order and product-specific terms.

Products should be returned in the condition required by the applicable return policy. Required accessories, packaging, labels, manuals, or other included components may need to be returned with the product.

3. Partial or Reduced Refund
A refund may be reduced or may not be issued in full if a returned product is damaged, used beyond the permitted condition, missing components, materially different from the item originally supplied, or otherwise fails the applicable return requirements.

The amount of any partial refund depends on the circumstances of the return and the applicable policy. Customers should package returned products securely and include all required components.

4. Refund After Return
When a return is required, the refund is processed after the returned item reaches the designated return location and the required checks have been completed. Processing time can vary depending on the return, verification, and payment method.

5. Refund Without Return
In certain circumstances, DemoKart may process a refund without requiring the customer to return the product. This may apply to specific products, situations, or resolutions where a return is not required.

6. Refund Method
Refunds are generally issued using the applicable payment or refund mechanism associated with the order. The time taken for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment method, financial institution, and processing timelines.

7. Refund for Cancelled Orders
If an order is cancelled after payment has been made, any applicable refund is processed according to the cancellation status and the payment method used for the order. The refund timeline may vary depending on the payment method and processing requirements.

8. Non-Returnable Products
Some products may not be eligible for standard returns because of their category, nature, health or safety considerations, customization, shipping restrictions, or other applicable conditions. However, a non-returnable product that arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from what was ordered may still qualify for a refund or replacement where the applicable policy permits.

9. Refund Processing Time
Refund processing consists of two stages: processing by DemoKart and processing by the payment provider or financial institution. After DemoKart initiates an eligible refund, the time for funds to become available can vary according to the payment method.

10. Customer Support
Customers with refund questions should contact DemoKart Customer Support through the Help Centre or the support option available for the relevant order. Customers should keep order details and return information available when contacting support.
